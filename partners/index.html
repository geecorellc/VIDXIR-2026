How credits work — worth knowing before you mail

Generation is metered in credits, because a cinematic 4K scene costs real provider money and a 720p one doesn't. Every tier gets a monthly allowance that resets each month — the Unlimited Bundle resets at 10,000. If a buyer burns through early and won't wait for the reset, top-up packs are one click away. That's the built-in repeat revenue after launch week.

Two paths to the same place: $37 → $497 Bundle (one decision, everything unlocked), or $37 → $97 → $67 → $197 stacked individually ($398). Price points step up during the 5-day window — lead with the front end and let the funnel do the rest.
